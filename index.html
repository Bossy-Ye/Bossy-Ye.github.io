Boshuai Ye

Doctoral Researcher, Quantum Software Engineering
M3S Research Unit, University of Oulu, Finland

I am a doctoral researcher in the M3S Empirical Software Engineering Research Unit at the University of Oulu, supervised by Assoc. Prof. Arif Ali Khan and co-supervised by Dr. Marko Paavola (VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland). My research develops the software engineering foundations for reliable quantum computing: toolchains that turn classical problem specifications into executable, hardware-aware quantum workflows; models of fault-tolerant execution under realistic resource constraints such as magic-state delivery; and audit methods that test whether empirical claims in quantum software remain stable under reproduction.

I lead the development of C2|Q⟩, a framework for compiling classical specifications into hybrid quantum workflows, published in ACM Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology (TOSEM, 2026), and of ClaimStab-QC, an audit framework for comparative claims in quantum software. Before my doctoral studies, I completed an M.Sc. (Tech.) with Honours in computer science at Aalto University, specialising in algorithms, logic, and computation, and in quantum technology.

Research

Research overview
  • Quantum workflow generation. Compiling classical problem specifications into executable, hardware-aware hybrid quantum workflows (C2|Q⟩, TOSEM 2026).
  • Fault-tolerant execution under resource constraints. Predicting execution slowdown when magic-state delivery, not circuit depth, bounds performance (IEEE QCE 2026).
  • Evidence and claim stability in quantum software. Auditing whether published empirical comparisons hold within explicit, reproducible evidence boundaries (ClaimStab-QC).

About me

I am a doctoral researcher in the M3S Empirical Software Engineering Research Unit at the University of Oulu, Finland, advised by Assoc. Prof. Arif Ali Khan and co-advised by Dr. Marko Paavola (VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland). I received my M.Sc. (Tech.) in Computer Science with Honours from Aalto University in 2024, and my B.Sc. from the University of Oulu in 2023.

Research

I work on reliable, resource-aware quantum software systems. My current work covers three problems: translating classical problem specifications into executable, hardware-aware quantum workflows; predicting fault-tolerant execution slowdown when magic-state delivery, not circuit depth, bounds performance; and auditing whether comparative claims in quantum software are supported by the evidence their sources expose. Papers are grouped by these topics on the publications page.

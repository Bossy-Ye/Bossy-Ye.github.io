Boshuai Ye

Doctoral Researcher · Quantum Software Engineering
University of Oulu, Finland

I study the engineering of reliable, resource-aware quantum software: translating classical problems into executable quantum workflows, modelling fault-tolerant execution under real resource constraints, and auditing the evidence behind comparative claims.

Email Scholar GitHub CV

Research

Research agenda
  • Workflow generation. Compiling classical problem specifications into executable, hardware-aware quantum workflows.
  • Resource-constrained execution. Predicting fault-tolerant slowdown when magic-state delivery, not circuit depth, bounds performance.
  • Evidence and claim auditing. Testing whether published comparative claims are supported by the evidence their sources expose.
